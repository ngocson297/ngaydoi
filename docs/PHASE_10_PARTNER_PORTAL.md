# Phase 10 — Partner Portal & Referral Commercialization

## Goal
Commercialize the partner channel for studios, wedding planners, venues and agencies without mixing partner data with customer ownership.

## Delivered
- Partner application, approval, suspension and rejection.
- Partner organization and role-based membership foundation.
- Customer/wedding attribution.
- Commission ledger with pending, approved, payable, paid and void states.
- Payout requests with masked account number and Admin review.
- White-label brand name and primary color foundation.
- Partner dashboard and Admin Partner Operations.
- Responsive hardening for 390px, 768px and desktop.

## Important controls
- A partner cannot add clients until approved.
- A customer must already have a Ngày Đôi account.
- A supplied wedding must belong to that customer.
- Only Owner/Manager can change branding or request payout.
- Payout cannot exceed payable balance or fall below the configured minimum.
- Full bank account numbers are not returned after payout creation.
